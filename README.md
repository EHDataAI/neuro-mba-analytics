# Neuro-MBA Analytics — Chile 2025

## Consumer Intelligence & Market Basket Analytics

A research and demonstration project combining
synthetic consumer behavior, Market Basket Analysis,
executive dashboards and business intelligence.

### Analytical capabilities

- Executive commercial analytics
- Product and simulated attention analysis
- Market Basket Analysis
- Basket economics
- Temporal analysis
- Campaign hypothesis generation

### Technology stack

Python, Streamlit, pandas, NumPy, Plotly, pytest.

### Scientific limitations

All demonstration data are synthetic.

TTFF and FFD metrics are simulated and do not represent
real eye-tracking measurements.

Basket associations are descriptive. The project does not
claim causal effects or validated commercial ROI.

### Engineering and quality

The project follows reproducibility, security,
software quality and methodological validation practices.

### Demonstration status

An interactive demonstration is undergoing a separate
security and intellectual property review.

The public repository excludes proprietary simulation
generators, experimental datasets, model implementations
and restricted decision logic.
